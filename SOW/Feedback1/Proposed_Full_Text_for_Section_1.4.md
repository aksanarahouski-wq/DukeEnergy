# Proposed Revised Language
## Acceptance Criteria for the Deliverables and Milestones

Deliverables shall be evaluated for production acceptance using the quality standards, defect severity definitions, and testing processes defined in **Section 1.5 (Project Governance)**.

Deliverables meet acceptance criteria when they:

#### Requirements Conformance:
- Substantially conform to requirements specified in the approved **Product Requirements Document (PRD)**, and **Development Backlog** as defined at commencement of development
- Achieve the core business objectives and user outcomes specified in the PRD

#### Quality Standards (per Section 1.5.6 Defect Severity Matrix):
- Are free of **Critical defects** (system unusable, security breach, data loss, core functionality completely broken)
- Are free of **High-severity defects** (major functionality broken, significant user impact), unless CLIENT waives specific High-severity defects in writing with acknowledgment of business impact
- **Medium and Low severity defects** (secondary issues, cosmetic problems, edge cases) do not block acceptance and will be added to the Development Backlog for prioritization per Section 1.2

#### Testing and Validation (per Section 1.5.5 Quality Assurance Process):
- Pass Orases' internal quality gates including unit testing, integration testing, and QA validation
- Complete **User Acceptance Testing (UAT)** with CLIENT Product Owner and Subject Matter Experts
- Validated in staging environment prior to production deployment

#### Technical Requirements:
- Meet security and compliance requirements as established by CLIENT cybersecurity team and validated during development
- Meet performance requirements as specified in the approved PRD/TRD
- Suitable for deployment to production environment in the intended infrastructure

#### Documentation:
- Include required documentation as specified in the PRD/TRD (architecture diagrams, configuration guides, deployment procedures, or other artifacts as applicable)

#### Substantial Conformance Standard:

The Parties acknowledge that in agile/iterative development, some implementation details, edge cases, and integration specifics emerge during development as requirements are translated into working software. When Orases encounters scenarios not explicitly addressed in the approved PRD/TRD, Orases will:

1. Notify CLIENT Product Owner of the scenario
2. Make reasonable decisions consistent with industry best practices and the spirit of the requirements
3. Document the approach and rationale

Decisions made in good faith to address unspecified scenarios constitute substantial conformance with requirements. If CLIENT prefers a different approach after review, modifications will be treated as enhancements and added to the Backlog for prioritization per Section 1.2, not grounds for rejection or non-acceptance.

#### Production Readiness:
- No known Critical or High-severity defects that would prevent safe production deployment
- Orases has validated the Deliverable in a staging environment that reasonably simulates the production environment
