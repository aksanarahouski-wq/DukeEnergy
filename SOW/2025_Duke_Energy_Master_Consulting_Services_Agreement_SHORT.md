Key Structure

  This is a master umbrella agreement with no guaranteed work. Actual projects come through:
  - SOW (Statement of Work) - detailed project descriptions you'll negotiate
  - PO (Purchase Orders) - Duke's administrative tracking documents

  Each SOW/PO is a separate commitment by a specific Duke Energy entity.

  ---
  Critical Business Terms

  Payment Terms (Section 2)

  - Net 90 days - Duke pays invoices 90 days after receipt (industry standard for utilities)
  - Must reference PO/Contract number on every invoice
  - Time-and-materials work requires detailed timesheets
  - Final invoice due within 60 days of project completion

  Fees & Rates (Exhibit B)

  - Rate schedule will be in Exhibit B (not yet filled in)
  - Rates locked for minimum period specified in Exhibit B
  - Once SOW accepted, rates can't change until SOW complete
  - Changes require 60 days written notice + written amendment (no oral modifications)

  Expenses (Exhibit C)

  - You're responsible for all expenses unless SOW says otherwise
  - Reimbursable expenses must follow Duke's Guidelines (Exhibit C)

  ---
  Major Legal Obligations

  1. Intellectual Property Ownership (Section 6)

  ⚠️ CRITICAL: Duke owns ALL deliverables
  - Everything you create is "work for hire" owned by Duke
  - All copyrights, patents, trademarks transfer to Duke
  - You must provide source code, data, graphics with deliverables
  - Pre-existing third-party code must come with perpetual, royalty-free licenses for Duke

  2. Warranties (Section 5)

  You warrant that:
  - Services meet professional standards with qualified personnel
  - Work conforms to specs and is free from defects
  - Software won't contain viruses, trojan horses, disabling code
  - No software infringement of third-party rights
  - Must re-perform or refund if warranties breached

  3. Inspection & Acceptance (Section 4)

  - Duke has 90 days to review/test deliverables
  - If rejected, you have 30 days to fix at your expense
  - If not fixed, Duke can fix it themselves and back-charge you OR terminate and get full refund

  ---
  Compliance Requirements

  Personnel & Background Screening (Section 7D)

  ⚠️ Important for staffing:
  - Must use E-Verify for employment eligibility
  - Minimum Screening Guidelines required for all personnel accessing Duke sites
  - Background checks, drug testing required
  - Must maintain employment records 3 years after hire / 1 year after termination

  Drug & Alcohol Policy (Section 7D3-4)

  - Must implement drug/alcohol policy as stringent as Duke's
  - CCS/COATS substance abuse testing for certain sites
  - SAMHSA 5-panel drug screen required
  - Random testing (5-15% quarterly)
  - "For cause" testing on reasonable suspicion
  - Positive test = immediate removal from site

  Safety & Security (Section 7B)

  - Must comply with Duke's EHS procedures
  - Follow all Duke safety protocols while on premises
  - Provide Material Safety Data Sheets for hazardous materials

  ---
  Confidentiality & Data Security (Sections 10.1 & 10.2)

  ⚠️ VERY STRICT REQUIREMENTS

  Confidential Information Handling:
  - No Duke data stored outside US without written consent
  - All Duke data must be encrypted at rest and in transit
  - PII must be encrypted with specific controls (NIST standards)
  - Masking required for data display
  - Cryptographic erasure required when returning/destroying data

  Security Program Requirements:
  - Must maintain comprehensive Security Program
  - Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) required for all systems with Duke data
  - Must follow OWASP Top 10, SANS Top 25 security practices
  - Annual SOC 2 Type 2 audit OR ISO 27001/IEC 62443 certification required

  Security Events (Breaches):
  - Must notify Duke within 24 hours of any security breach
  - Within 30 minutes for critical cyber systems
  - You pay for ALL notification costs, credit monitoring, forensic investigations
  - Must provide detailed incident report within 10 business days

  Network Access:
  - Vendor Network (internet access) vs Internal Network (Duke systems)
  - Strict monitoring - no gambling, pornography, hate sites
  - Duke can terminate access anytime

  ---
  Change Management (Section 8)

  - Changes require written Change Orders signed by both parties
  - Must notify Duke of fee/schedule impact BEFORE Change Order execution
  - In emergencies, Duke can issue written change directive
  - Work outside SOW scope = no payment claim

  ---
  Termination Rights (Section 9)

  Duke Can Terminate:

  1. For Convenience - anytime, any reason with written notice
    - You get paid for work done + reasonable termination expenses
    - NO profit on unperformed work, NO other damages
  2. For Cause - with 10 days to cure
    - Includes: bankruptcy, law violations, policy violations
    - Duke gets all remedies at law
  3. Suspension - Duke can temporarily suspend work anytime

  Must comply with numerous federal regulations including:
  - Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO)
  - Veterans employment (VEVRAA)
  - Affirmative Action for Disabilities
  - Fair Labor Standards Act
  - Anti-trafficking in persons
  - Executive Order 13658 (minimum wages)

  2. 90-day inspection period
  3. Termination for convenience (lose profit on unperformed work)

  - Most Fortune 500 utilities have similar MSAs
